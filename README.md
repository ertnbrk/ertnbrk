## 🚀 About Me

I'm a **Software Engineer** from **Istanbul, Turkey**, currently in my final year of **Software Engineering at Doğuş University**.  
With a strong foundation in both **AI-driven systems** and **modern backend architecture**, I design and build solutions that merge research-level AI with scalable production environments.

I’ve worked on **.NET microservices**, **cloud deployments**, and **AI-powered applications** — from **voice-based assistants** and **image recognition** to **cleanly architected backend platforms**.  

- 💡 Passionate about **Distributed Systems, Cloud, and AI engineering**  
- 🌱 Currently exploring **MLOps, FinOps**, and **Cloud computing**  
- 👯 Open to collaborating on **AI-powered SaaS & Cloud-Native Projects**  
- ⚡ Motto: *“Build smart, scale efficiently.”*
